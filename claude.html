Rentkammer Case Files: Presentation Guide

This guide covers the seven Rentkammer case files in sphinx-nla (1237, 1291, 139, 146, 147, 188, 689). The general documents (140, 2713, 2728, 2741) are not case files and are not covered.

What the case files do now

The content is strong. The presentation differs from file to file in five ways.

Where a case file begins. 1237, 1291 and 689 open on the Designatio Actorum (content.md). 139, 146 and 188 open on an index.md with a narrative. 147 opens on its cover. 1291, 147 and 188 have no narrative overview at all. The narrative material that does exist is spread across several kinds of page:

  • intro.md in 1237 and 689
  • timeline.md in 1237 and 139
  • amt-decree.md in 689
  • two analyses of Document 1 in 146, placed ahead of the cover

Order of pages. In 139 the timeline comes before the cover. In 146 the analyses come before the cover. In 689 the overview of the Amt decree comes before Document 1.

The Designatio Actorum. 1237 and 139 use list-table. The others use flat-table. The columns differ from file to file. The heading is spelled four ways: “Designatio Actorum”, “Designation Actorum” (1291, 689), “Contents List” and “Table of Contents”.

Document pages. The introductory section has seven names: Overview, Summary, Narrative Overview, Narrative Summary, Document Summary, Explanation and Comments. Almost every page in 1291 and 188 has none. Image sections have four names: Image, Images, Digital Image and Digital Images. Several pages also have empty wrapper headings such as “Transliteration and Translation” (sometimes misspelled “Transliateration”). A few pages in 689 put transliteration and translation side by side in a list-table. Everywhere else they come one after the other in doc-text boxes. Titles carry a date in 139 and 1237, carry none in 1291, and in the 1850 pages of 146 put the date first.

Documents with several parts. A docket page, the petition and a marginal resolution are all handled with H2 headings. As a result, “Transliteration” and “Translation” appear several times at the same level, and the page’s right-hand outline becomes a column of identical entries.

Glossaries are also repeated page by page (1237, 139, 1291/doc22), although rentkammer/terms.md already exists. Finally, apart from 140, none of the pages carries an archival citation.

Your 139 document pages, such as jh-krueckeberg-petition-to-sell.md, come closest to the pattern recommended below. 1291 and 188 are furthest from it.

Recommended structure for a case file

Every case file should have the same sequence in _toc.yml:

  1. index.md: the landing page and chapter entry (layout below)
  2. cover.md: the case file cover
  3. content.md: the Designatio Actorum
  4. Documents, in Designatio order
  5. Later documents, if any (as in 139 and 146), grouped under their own short overview page
  6. Supplementary studies, if any (the doc1 analyses in 146, the numbering notes in 689), placed last and not ahead of the cover

Give every document a title: in _toc.yml in the same form: Type of document, who (date). For example: Petition of the widow Krückeberg (8 Jul 1824).

## Timeline

Optional. Use one only when the file covers more than one episode or its
documents are out of date order. Keep it to a compressed table, with each row
linking to its document.

## Citation

Niedersächsisches Landesarchiv, Abteilung Bückeburg, NLA BU, L 101a, A Nr. 139,
"German case-file title" [English title], 1824–1849.

## Notes

Translation notes, docket marks and numbering observations (optional).

Use only these headings, in this order: Overview, Images, Text, Notes. Leave out a section when it has nothing in it, rather than renaming it.

The tabs come from sphinx_design, which _config.yml already loads but no page uses yet. Putting Translation in the first tab means the reader lands on the English. Anyone checking your reading of the German is one click away from the transliteration. The page also stays half as long, which matters for the many two- and three-page documents. The side-by-side tables in 689 should be changed to this same form.

Documents with several parts

Write the overview once, at the top. Then give each physical part its own H2 heading, with Images and Text as H3 headings inside it:

## Overview

## Part 1: Docket page
### Images
### Text

## Part 2: Petition
### Images
### Text

## Part 3: Marginal resolution of the Rentkammer
### Images
### Text

The right-hand outline then reads “Docket page / Petition / Marginal resolution” instead of six identical “Transliteration / Translation” entries.

Glossary

Move the per-page glossaries into rentkammer/terms.md and make it a MyST {glossary}. Pages can then link a term with {term}`Brinksitzer`, and each definition is kept in one place. Admonitions remain the right tool for an explanation that only matters on one page.

Order of work
