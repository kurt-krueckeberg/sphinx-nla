## Translation

```{div} doc-text
Draft\
Re: Chamber No. 368.

Chamber rescript to the Office of Bückeburg.

Dispatched by Schwarze.

By the Grace of God.

The designation of land for the tenant Jobst Heinrich Krückeberg,
No. 18 at Bärenbusch, together with the dues (Prästanden) proposed by
you, has been approved. These dues are to be paid for the
first time at Michaelmas 1813. We etc.

Bückeburg, 15 November 1809.

On behalf of [the Prince]\
von Danckwertz von Lindesberg\
M. Kaas


Chamber instruction to the Chamber Rent Master Staackmann.

Dispatched.

To the new settler Jobst Heinrich Krückeberg, No. 18 at Bärenbusch,
102 square Ruten 51 square feet have been designated for building
a house. The Chamber Rent Master is hereby instructed to collect
the purchase price of this designated plot, namely 6 mariengroschen
per square Rute, and to enter it as income at the proper place.

Bückeburg, 15 [½?] November 1809.\
Princely Schaumburg-Lippe [Rent Chamber?]\
von Danckwertz von Lindesberg\
M. Kaas

‡ That is, in sum: 17 Reichsthaler 3 gl.
```
