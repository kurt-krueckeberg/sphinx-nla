# Sale of the Krückeberg property no. 18 in Berenbusch to the shoemaker

Only the case file cover and the Abschrift (copy) of
[N.C. 4386](../146/carl-fried-supplication-to-sell.md) from  case file [Holding No. 18
in Berenbusch (1808-1810 & Sept. 1850)](../146/index.md) are included here. All the
other documents include technical details of the sale. They are hard to
decipher due to their poor legibility.

The really important details are found in case file [Holding No. 18
in Berenbusch (1808-1810 & Sept. 1850)](../146/index.md), in its [Other
Documents](../146/other-docs.md) section, which contains all the 
relevant details of the September 1850 sale of no. 18 Berenbusch.
