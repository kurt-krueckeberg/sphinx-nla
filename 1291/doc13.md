## Translation

```{div} doc-text
Document 13.

No. 372

[receipt / acknowledgment]

That the Colon Vogt No. 13 in Nordholz has paid forty-nine Reichsthaler
into the Princely Chamber treasury for an assigned parcel of land is hereby
acknowledged.

Berger

Bückeburg, 14 September 1818

49 Reichsthaler
```
