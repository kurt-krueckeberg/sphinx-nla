# New Settler Holding of Triftmeier, No. 19 in Berenbusch (1819-1820)

## Overview

This case file concerns the lodger (Einlieger) Triftmeier, who was granted a
building plot in the Baumer forest for the new settler holding no. 19 in
Berenbusch. Only [Document 5](doc5.md) is included here, because it is the
only document that mentions **Krückeberg**.

```{list-table}
:widths: 25 75

* - **Date span**
  - 1819–1820
* - **Holding**
  - New settler holding (Neubauer-Stätte) No. 19, Berenbusch (Triftmeier)
* - **Document included**
  - [Report of the offices of Bückeburg and Arensburg](doc5.md), 29 October 1819
* - **Outcome**
  - Rentkammer approved the offices' proposal on 22 November 1819
* - **Genealogical value**
  - Dates the settlement of Krückeberg at no. 18 Berenbusch to about 1808
* - **Map**
  - {ref}`Survey drawing by Stille <krueckeberg-no-18-garten>`, showing the
    location of Krückeberg's garden at no. 18
```

## Summary of Document 5

On 29 October 1819 the offices of Bückeburg and Arensburg reported to the
Rentkammer that the plot surveyed for Triftmeier measured 1 Morgen, 54
square Ruthen, 80 square feet. The local assessors (Achtsleute) had valued it
at 13 to 15 Groschen per square Ruthe.

The offices set that valuation aside because of what Krückeberg had paid:

> Krückeberg of No. 18 in Berenbusch, who settled eleven years ago
> immediately adjacent to Triftmeier's plot on land of equally good quality,
> was graciously granted his entire plot of 102 square Ruthen and 51 square
> feet at a price of 6 Groschen per square Ruthe.

Krückeberg's price was decisive. His plot lay next to Triftmeier's and had
the same soil, and he is the only settler the report names as a precedent.
On that basis the offices recommended 6 Groschen per square Ruthe for the
first half Morgen and about 12 Groschen for the rest, well below the
assessors' figures. The Rentkammer approved the recommendation unchanged on
22 November 1819.

## Genealogical Significance

The statement that Krückeberg settled at no. 18 "eleven years ago" is the key
genealogical information in this case file. Counted back from October 1819,
it places his settlement in about 1808.

This agrees with the case file for [Site No. 18 in Berenbusch](../146/index.md),
in which the tailor Krückeberg [petitioned for a building plot](../146/doc1.md)
on 29 September 1808 and received the same 102 square Ruthen 51 square feet.

## Map Showing Krückeberg's Garden

[Document 5](doc5.md) includes a {ref}`survey drawing <krueckeberg-no-18-garten>`
by the surveyor Stille that shows where Krückeberg's garden lay. It is
labelled "Krückeberg Nr. 18 Garten", next to the house marked "Kolonie Nr. 18",
on the far side of the road from Berenbusch to Evesen and Petzen, opposite
Triftmeier's plot.
