# Site No. 10 in Berenbusch (1824-1849)

## Overview

The Krückeberg remained Brinksitzers of No. 10 Berenbusch from at least
1743-1744 until September of 1849 when Johann Heinrich (1806-1879) sold his
inheritable tenancy right to Wilhelm Bornemann of Nordholtz.

The case file primarily concerns the petition of Widow Krückeberg of no.
10 Berenbusch before the Rentkammer, in 8 July 1824, to be relieved of the
feudal obligations of manual labor that accompanied being a Stätte holder.
She was 58 years old and could not do the manual labor herself and her late 
husband’s poor health had impoverished her because of medical expenses
spent on physicians and medicines.

There are also a series of documents from August and September of 1849 in
which "Colon Krückeberg", which is Johann Heinrich (1806-1879) petitions
the Rentkammer to accept the sale of his holding to Wilhelm Boremann
because he is immigrating to American, and the ship for America departs on
[September 15, 1849](jh-krueckeberg-petition-to-sell.md).

    joint effort to save him has left the petitioner entirely impoverished. She
    therefore deserves approval of any relief that might ease her condition.

  - The Rentcammer resolves to grant the widow Krückeberg exemption from the
